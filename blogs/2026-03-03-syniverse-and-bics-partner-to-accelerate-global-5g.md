Syniverse and BICS Partner to Accelerate Global 5G Standalone Roaming TAMPA, Fla. — Mar. 3, 2026 — Syniverse, the world’s most connected company® and a global leader in international connectivity, and BICS, a Proximus Global Company and leading digital communications enabler, have partnered to expand the reach of 5G Standalone (SA) roaming.
