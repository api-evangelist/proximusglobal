Real-time Anti-fraud Intelligence solution helps identify fake and potentially fraudulent accounts, keeping online shopping seamless and secure for everyday users. Brussels – August 13th, 2026 – Telesign, a Proximus Global company and a leader in digital identity and programmable communications, has partnered with Bancontact Company, Belgium’s dominant domestic payment company. The partnership integrates Telesign’s newly launched Anti-fraud Intelligence platform to deliver real-time risk insights, reinforcing security of the Bancontact Pay app.
